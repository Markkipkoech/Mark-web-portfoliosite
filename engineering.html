Applied engineering

CompanyHub — practical engineering work

Through CompanyHub, I am applying backend engineering principles to a business management platform.

Areas of implementation include:

  • Backend architecture: Organizing application functionality into modular components with clear responsibilities.
  • Domain services: Keeping business rules and operations within dedicated service layers.
  • APIs and access control: Developing backend APIs and controlling access to business resources.
  • Data integrity: Applying database constraints and business rules to protect important records.
  • Financial calculations: Building consistent calculations for sales and related business operations.
  • Concurrency: Addressing competing operations that could affect sales records and business data.
  • Testing: Writing automated tests for business logic, API behavior and database integrity.

My engineering approach

I aim to understand the problem before choosing a solution. I pay attention to business rules, ownership, permissions, invalid operations, data consistency, failure handling and testing before treating an implementation as complete.

I value maintainable solutions, clear boundaries, deliberate technical decisions and evidence that the system behaves correctly.
